Third-party software in the Chargewire JDBC driver's jar
=========================================================

The driver's jar is standalone: it contains the components below, most of them moved to packages
under com.gizmodata.chargewire.jdbc.shaded. The driver itself is licensed under the Apache License
2.0 (META-INF/LICENSE); its notice, followed by the notices these components carry, is
META-INF/NOTICE.

Each line names a component as groupId:artifactId:version, its licence (SPDX identifier), and
where that licence's text is in this jar. The build checks this list against what the jar really
contains (ThirdPartyNoticesTest, JarLicensesIT).

Software inside those components, under licences of its own:

- Zstandard, whose source zstd-jni compiles into its native libraries (the aix, darwin, freebsd,
  linux and win directories of this jar): BSD-3-Clause, META-INF/licenses/zstd/LICENSE. Zstandard
  is offered under that licence or the GNU General Public License version 2; it is distributed
  here under the BSD licence. The text is the one zstd-jni 1.5.7-23 carries with that source.
- FastDoubleParser, inside jackson-core: MIT, META-INF/FastDoubleParser-LICENSE; the code it
  includes in turn is described in META-INF/FastDoubleParser-ThirdParty-LICENSE.
- Schubfach, inside jackson-core: MIT, META-INF/Schubfach-LICENSE.

Where the licence texts come from: the Apache License 2.0 text is the one the Arrow, Jackson,
Commons and FlatBuffers distributions carry; the SLF4J text is the licence file inside the
slf4j-api and slf4j-nop jars of the version above; the zstd-jni and Zstandard texts are the files
LICENSE and src/main/native/LICENSE of https://github.com/luben/zstd-jni at tag v1.5.7-23 (its jar
carries none); the FastDoubleParser and Schubfach texts are jackson-core's own files.
